Morgan Stanley · 2024–2026

One source of truth for client data

Six teams each had their own way to bring in client data from internal systems and vendors such as Bloomberg, GLEIF and Markit. Every new source meant new code, and the same client could show up as different records in different places.

RoleTechnical lead and project manager · 9 engineers · 10–30 stakeholders

What I built

A configuration-driven framework: each feed is declared in YAML, validated by a Spring Boot service, and generated into Airflow workflows that infer their own task dependencies. Data lands in a medallion pipeline on Azure, Databricks and Snowflake. Quantexa entity resolution gives every client one firm-wide ID.

How I delivered it

I turned six teams’ workflows into one onboarding model, set up two-week sprints and an intake process, and reported to Executive and Managing Directors every two weeks. When a client couldn’t wait, we served them through existing components until their source was scheduled.

  • 8sources onboarded in 5 months
  • 50%processing efficiency gain
  • 0open production tickets for a year

Morgan Stanley · 2021–2023

Grouping clients for a regulator

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority required the bank’s Asia entities to track credit exposure per group of connected clients: those under common control or ownership. That logic had to sit inside account opening, which five other teams also owned.

RoleTechnical lead · 12 engineers · 5 partner teams

What I built

A grouping calculation engine and approval workflow embedded in the wealth-management account-opening flow, with results written to the account master. In the same role, I automated client-committee and fast-track approvals as event-driven Spring Boot and Kafka services.

How I delivered it

I managed dependencies across the frontend, QA, production engineering, Pega workflow and client teams, and planned the cutover, rollback and release coordination for go-live. I also ramped up new graduate engineers on the team.

  • Liveregulatory requirement met in production
  • 50%+less manual effort in approvals
  • 2022Operations Technology Award, Taking Ownership

Morgan Stanley · 2023–2024

From five months to three weeks

Adding one field to non-client account onboarding took 4–5 months. The platform was undocumented legacy JavaScript, and the reasons for the delay weren’t obvious.

RoleTechnical lead · multiple business units

What I built

A target microservice architecture and a migration path for the undocumented platform. In the same role, I built Pipex Initializer, a tool that standardises how new microservices are created in the monorepo.

How I delivered it

I traced the delay to dependent components missing from test environments and code that was hard to debug. Then I agreed a plan with stakeholders: new requests move first, and the full platform follows.

  • 2–3 wkper attribute, down from 4–5 months
  • 5 minservice setup, down from 1–2 days
  • 40+services built with Pipex
