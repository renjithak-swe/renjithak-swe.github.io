What I’m exploring, and where it comes from

I’m interested in what happens when data systems feed automated decisions. A pipeline can run, a dataset can pass validation, and the answer can still be unfit for the decision. I explore how to make these systems more transparent and trustworthy, in small open experiments anyone can check.

Clean but unfit

Can a dataset pass quality checks and still be unable to answer a reasonable question?

On the widely used California Housing dataset, two of five questions had a reliable answer. The other three depended on values hidden above a cap, and two of them swung by 60–70% across what-if scenarios.

Field notes: how I designed and delivered it

Engineering. I designed the configuration-driven framework, so new sources are added by writing configuration, not code. Then I led the team that built the pipeline on Azure, Databricks and Snowflake.

Delivery. I merged six teams’ workflows into one onboarding model, ran two-week sprints, and reported to Executive and Managing Directors every two weeks.
